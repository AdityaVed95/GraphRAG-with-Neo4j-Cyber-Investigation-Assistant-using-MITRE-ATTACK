- **Group:** A named group of attackers, such as **APT29**.
- **Software:** A tool or malware attackers use, such as **Mimikatz**.
- **Technique:** **How** attackers perform an action, such as stealing passwords from memory. Sub-techniques describe more specific methods.
- **Tactic:** **Why** attackers perform an action—their goal, such as gaining access or stealing credentials.
- **Mitigation:** A defensive action that reduces the risk of a technique succeeding.
- **Campaign:** A set of related attacker activities carried out over a particular period toward shared goals.

Together, they answer: **Which group used which software and techniques, for what purpose, during which campaign, and what defenses could help?**
